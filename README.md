</td>

Cash in bank, net burn, revenue, expenses, net profit and break-even revenue, each with a trend line.

</td>

Actual and projected cash balance with a three-month buffer line and the cash-out point marked.

</td>

Category spend with fixed or variable tags and change versus the previous period. Click a row to open its entries.

</td>

Monthly income, expense and net profit or loss across 3, 6 or 12 months.
